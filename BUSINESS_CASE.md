# Business Case Study — Community Intelligence

## Business challenge

Digital community stories are dispersed across conversations.

## Solution

CSV/JSON ingestion, LLM-assisted extraction, traceable quotations, human curation and OCI storage.

## Competencies demonstrated

Project management, acceptance criteria, stakeholder coordination, AI governance.

## Documented evidence

The 22 November 2026 historical record describes a demonstration with fictitious data, LLM analysis and OCI storage. Functional acceptance remained pending in that record.

## Scope and limitations

Collaborative project; my role was Project Manager. No automatic publication or measured commercial impact.
